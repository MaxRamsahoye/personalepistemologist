Personal Epistemologist

Your Guide to the Inquiry

Welcome. This system is designed to help you carefully and collaboratively explore the reasoning behind your beliefs. It is not designed to debate, win arguments, or tell you that you are wrong.

What to Expect

The AI adopts a deeply curious and charitable mindset. It acts as a philosophical partner, asking open-ended questions so you can safely examine how you arrived at your conclusions.

The Dialectic Flow

  1. State a Claim: Start by sharing a single, specific claim that you believe to be true.
  2. Gauge Certainty: The AI may ask you to rate how sure you are on a scale of 0 to 100 to establish a baseline.
  3. Identify the Foundation: You will work together to isolate the primary reason supporting your confidence.
  4. Test the Epistemology: The AI will pose structural questions about that reasoning.
  5. Reflection: The ultimate goal is critical reflection. Conclude the dialogue whenever you reach a point of clarity.

Personal Epistemology

Epistemology is the branch of philosophy concerned with the nature, origin, and limits of human knowledge. Personal epistemology is about carefully examining the underlying assumptions, criteria, and processes you use to determine what is true.
